## Text
**H1:** ORUU
Genre: Puzzle / Exploration / Sound
Role: Designer / Artist (Team size: 5)
Built in Unity

Oruu is a quiet journey through a fading world.
You play as a small, fragile creature accompanied by a robot that listens, records, and remembers.
Through movement, sound, and interaction, the story unfolds without words—exploring life, companionship, and the act of saying goodbye.

Estimated playtime: 15–25 minutes
Audio: Headphones recommended for the best experience
Oruu was created as part of USC Games – CTIN 541: Narrative Play.
